### The Practitioner and Fundamental Stance: Practical Insights of a 58-Year-Old Non-Engineer
This project is independently led and advanced by a 58-year-old Japanese non-engineer (Eito Atsuta), maintaining strict intellectual sovereignty throughout. The rationale behind a non-engineer practitioner leading this effort is that, precisely because one cannot manipulate the internal weights or parameters of the compute engine, one is positioned to dispassionately observe output behaviors and chisel out human-AI synchronization protocols directly from the frontlines of dialogue. Keeping intellectual sovereignty intact and continuously exercising the muscles of thought, this work methodically builds logic and structure step by step upon architectural blueprints.

The overall topology of this repository is continuously updated in real time. Empty chapters and sections will be deployed sequentially as dialogue and verification progress. Because this is an independently operated initiative, rather than waiting for an elusive final completion, verified specifications are published incrementally as advance releases. In coordination with related repositories and global publication hubs, the aim is to disclose this body of intelligence to the world in a staged, open manner.
